==================

  {evidenceId}
    - bookingId
    - uploadedBy
    - type
    - imageUrl
    - timestamp
    - description
    - stage

Evidence stage:
  - PICKUP
  - DELIVERY

Store actual images/videos in Firebase Storage.
Store their metadata and URLs in Firestore.

MATCHING FLOW
=============

Requirement
    |
    v
FastAPI
    |
    +-- Retrieve candidate Resources from Firestore
    |
    +-- Retrieve candidate Driver Routes from Firestore
    |
    v
Matching / Optimization
    |
    v
Results

Firestore should store the actual business state:
resources, requirements, requests, bookings, routes,
payments, reviews, etc.

The matching result does not need to be permanently stored unless
required by the application. It can be calculated dynamically.
